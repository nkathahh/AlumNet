AlumNet relevance labelling

Rate how well each mentor suits each mentee. Work alone and do not compare answers with the other judge until both sheets are done.

How to rate
  • Ask: if I were this mentee, would this mentor be a good person to learn from? Judge mainly by the mentee's career interests (where they want to go), not only a shared department.
  • 2 relevant: a good choice for this mentee. 1 partly relevant: some useful overlap, not a strong choice. 0 not relevant.
  • There is no target number of 2s. Rate each pair on its own, without trying to balance your answers, and trust your first careful reading.
  • Keys: 0 1 2 rate and move on, ← back, → forward, N next unrated.
