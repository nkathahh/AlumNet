# AlumNet user guide

AlumNet connects current Kenya Airways staff (**mentees**) with former staff (**mentors**)
for mentorship, events and career opportunities. The alumni office runs it (**admins**).
Live site: https://alumnet.pages.dev

## Everyone

**Sign up.** Open **Sign up** and enter your **staff number** and email.
- If the email is the one HR holds for you (work email for current staff, personal email for
  former staff), you get a sign-up link by email. Open it and choose a password, or use Google.
- If it is not, your request goes to the alumni office, who check it against HR records and
  may phone the number HR holds. You get an email when it is approved.
- Your role comes from HR: current staff are mentees, former staff are mentors.

**Finish your profile.** After signing in you are asked for your headline, skills,
experience and career interests. Matching uses only these, so be specific ("cargo load
planning, dangerous goods" rather than "operations").

**Sign in.** Email and password, or Google. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password**
on the sign-in page.

**Two-factor sign-in.** In **Account settings**, turn on two-factor with an authenticator app
and keep the backup codes somewhere safe. Admins must turn it on.

**Your data.** In **Account settings** you can **Download my data** or **Delete my account**.
You will be asked to accept the privacy notice again whenever it changes.

**Event photos.** Under **Alumni events > Past events**, open an event to see its highlights
full screen (arrow keys or swipe to move, Download to keep one) and the link to the full
Google Photos album. If you attended, you can add photos; the alumni office checks them first.
Photos the alumni office adds are credited to the KQ Alumni Office.

**Staying safe.** Mentoring on AlumNet is free: never pay anyone. Use **Report** on any
profile, post or event that looks wrong, and block anyone who bothers you.

## Mentees

1. **Find a mentor.** Matches are listed with a fit label (Strong fit, Good fit or Some
   overlap) and three bars showing how well skills, experience and goals match. Mentors you
   saved, or who fit events you joined, can move up slightly, and the card says why.
2. **Save** mentors to your shortlist to compare them. You can have 5 requests waiting at once.
3. **Request** a mentor with a short introduction: what you do and what guidance you want.
   Their email is shown only after they accept. A request with no reply expires after 14 days.
4. When a mentor accepts, open the **Workspace** from **My requests**: agree goals, tick
   them off and log each meeting.
5. When you finish, choose **End mentorship** and answer the short private questions.

## Mentors

1. On **My profile**, say whether you are taking mentees and how many (up to your limit).
2. **Mentee requests** shows how many free places you have, then each request with the
   mentee's goals, skills, what you have in common and how many days are left to reply.
   Search, filter by department and sort (expiring soonest first by default). Accept or
   decline; only accepting shares contact details. Tick several requests to decline them
   together with one short note. Accept is switched off once your places are full.
3. Use **Open workspace** for each mentee to agree goals and log meetings.
4. **Post an opportunity** on Opportunities or **Propose an event** on Alumni events. Posts are
   checked by the alumni office before they appear.
5. After an event you organised, open it under **Past events**: add the share link of a Google
   Photos album with every photo, upload the best 6 to 12 as highlights and choose the cover.

## Admins (alumni office)

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Approve sign-ups that need checking | **Identity checks** |
| Review posts and events | **Posts to review**, **Events** |
| Handle reports | **Reports** |
| Suspend, reactivate or reset two-factor for a member | **Members** |
| Add or remove admins (a second admin approves) | **Admins** |
| Import HR records (preview, then approve) | **HR records** |
| See requests, meetings and how evenly mentors are used | **Impact** |
| Every admin action, filterable and exportable | **Activity log** |
| Organisation logo | **Branding** |

Before resetting someone's two-factor, phone the number HR holds, never a number the caller
gives you.

## Known limitations

- Matching works in English only, and needs the three profile fields filled in.
- If the matching service is starting up, search uses keywords and says so.
- On the free hosting plans the first visit after a quiet period can take up to a minute.
- During the demonstration all emails go to the developer's inbox, because the HR records
  are synthetic.
