Identity verification

Sign-ups whose email did not match HR records. Each one is compared with HR for you; lowest risk first. If unsure, phone the number HR holds, not the one entered.

Reports

Profiles and posts members have reported. Money requests, harassment and fake profiles come first, then anything reported by several people. A post reported by three people is hidden until you review it.

HR records

Upload the latest staff list from Kenya Airways HR. Roles update from it: staff marked former become mentors and their open mentee requests are closed.

Import a CSV file

Columns: staff_number, full_name, department, kq_email, personal_email, mobile_number, employment_status, alumni_eligible. Status is active or former. You see the changes before anything is saved.
