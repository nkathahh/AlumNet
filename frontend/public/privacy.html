KQ AlumNet

Privacy notice

How AlumNet collects and uses your personal data, under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Who we are

AlumNet is the Kenya Airways alumni mentorship platform, run by the Kenya Airways alumni office. It connects current staff (mentees) with former staff (mentors).

What we use from Kenya Airways HR

To confirm you are, or were, Kenya Airways staff, we check the staff number and email you enter against HR records. From HR we use your name, department, work or personal email, whether you are current or former staff and whether you are eligible for the alumni network. Your role on AlumNet comes from this record, not from a choice you make. National ID numbers are never shown or stored in your AlumNet account.

What you give us

  • Your password (stored only as a one-way hash) or a linked Google account.
  • Profile details you choose to add: headline, about, skills, experience, career interests, location and LinkedIn link.
  • If we cannot verify you automatically: your name and phone number, used only by the alumni office to check your identity, then deleted.
  • Mentorship requests, event registrations and opportunities you post.

Why we use it

  • To verify who you are and keep impostors out.
  • To recommend mentors whose skills and experience fit your goals. Matching uses only your skills, experience and career interests.
  • To send account emails such as sign-up links, password resets and mentorship updates.
  • To keep the platform safe: admins see an activity log of their own actions, and suspicious sign-ups are reviewed.

Who can see what

  • Mentor profiles are visible to signed-in members. Mentee profiles are visible only to mentors they contact and to the alumni office.
  • Your email and phone number are never shown on your profile.
  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-up links expire after 24 hours.
  • Sign-up and verification records, including any name and phone number collected, are deleted 30 days after they are completed, rejected or expire.
  • Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.
  • If you delete your account, your profile and personal details are removed straight away.

Your rights

You can see and correct your profile at any time, download a copy of your data and delete your account from Account settings. For anything else, including correcting your HR record, contact the Kenya Airways alumni office.
